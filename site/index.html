Features

Everything for a walk with your phone in your pocket

Listen to the city instead of scrolling it. The app knows where you are and tells you what is around.

Narration right when you arrive

A foreground service tracks your position and starts the description when you enter a point's radius — even with the screen off. Radius is adjustable from 5 to 100 metres.

Descriptions written by AI

Generate text through OpenRouter — pick the model and edit the prompt template. The OSM object, address, city and country are injected automatically so the model never confuses places that share a name.

A map that gets out of the way

MapLibre with OpenFreeMap vector tiles: readable labels, radius circles, and a point card with elevation plus Play, Pause and Stop controls.

Routes and history

Group points into walks, rename or delete routes, and filter the map by route. History shows what you listened to and when — and lets you replay it.

Import and export

Back up routes and points with descriptions to JSON — move them to another device or restore after a reinstall. Settings export separately.

Your data stays yours

No account and no cloud: points, routes and history live on the device, while keys and tokens sit in encrypted storage. Only the services you choose receive requests.

How it works

Three steps to your first story

  1. 01

    Install the APK

    Download the file, allow installation from this source, then connect the services: a Yandex SpeechKit authorised key for voice and OpenRouter for generating text.

  2. 02

    Drop your points

    Long-press the map or use the “Point” button to add one next to you. Write the description by hand or generate it with AI, then set the trigger radius.

  3. 03

    Walk and listen

    Turn tracking on and pocket your phone. Each point starts speaking on its own — once, and again after you clear its history entry.

FAQ

The short answers

Is the app free?

The app and its source code are free. Voice and AI descriptions run through your own Yandex Cloud and OpenRouter accounts, which are billed separately.

Do I need an internet connection?

Points, routes and already generated audio work offline. Loading map tiles, generating new descriptions and synthesising new voice need a connection.

Why does it need background location?

To narrate while the screen is off, the app runs a foreground service with a persistent notification. Without background location permission, triggers will be missed.

Is there an iOS or Google Play version?

Android APK from GitHub Releases only, for now. Updates install over the previous version and keep your data and settings.

What language is the app in?

The app interface is currently in Russian, while narration depends on the Yandex SpeechKit voice you pick. This site is available in English and Russian.
